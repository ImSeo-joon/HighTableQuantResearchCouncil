      06 APPLICATION-TO-OFFER LAB

      THE FULL HIRING CHAIN

      From screen to superday

      A five-stage preparation system for written tests, technical interviews, cases, behavioral rounds and final-round decisions. Choose a stage to see what is tested and how to prepare.

      STAGE / 01

      Make the first ten seconds specific.

      The first screen is usually about evidence, relevance and role fit—not a list of every activity you have ever joined.

      WHAT IS TESTED
      • Role fit and a credible reason for choosing the division
      • Quantified impact, leadership and commercial curiosity
      • Accuracy, structure and attention to detail
      BUILD THIS WEEK
      • One page: evidence first, adjectives second
      • Prepare three deal, research or leadership stories with numbers
      • Write a firm-specific answer: why this team, why now?
      STAGE / 02

      Train for speed without sacrificing logic.

      Online assessments vary by employer and role. Treat every format as a test of calibrated assumptions, not just arithmetic.

      COMMON FORMATS
      • Numerical, logical and financial literacy tests
      • Situational judgment and workplace-priority tests
      • Coding, modeling or market-based simulations
      PREPARATION SYSTEM
      • Take timed sets, then review every wrong answer by failure type
      • For SJT, identify the employer's stated values and the least-harm action
      • Practice explaining code or calculations aloud after submission
      STAGE / 03

      Show how you think when the answer is not memorized.

      Technical rounds reward clean assumptions, explicit trade-offs and the ability to move between a model, a market and a decision.

      BY ROLE
      • IBD: accounting, valuation, DCF, merger consequences
      • Markets and quant: probability, pricing, market intuition, coding
      • Risk and compliance: regulations, scenarios, control design
      DELIVERY RULE
      • State assumptions before calculations
      • Use a simple base case, then a sensitivity
      • End with a recommendation and its main failure mode
      STAGE / 04

      Structure the problem. Protect the judgment.

      Case and behavioral interviews test whether you can prioritize under incomplete information without losing commercial or ethical judgment.

      CASE FORMATS
      • Investment case, deal discussion or market thesis
      • Profitability, market entry or operational improvement
      • Risk event, compliance dilemma or group simulation
      ANSWER SHAPE
      • Clarify the decision, scope and success metric
      • Use a hypothesis tree and prioritize two branches
      • Close with a recommendation, risk and next action
      STAGE / 05

      Consistency wins the final room.

      A superday or assessment center brings several interviews, group tasks and networking into one high-pressure sequence. Energy management is part of the test.

      FINAL-ROUND SIGNALS
      • Your story stays consistent across every interviewer
      • You listen, adapt and make others better in a group
      • Your questions reflect genuine commercial curiosity
      AFTER THE INTERVIEW
      • Record which questions exposed weak evidence
      • Clarify offer terms, location, visa and start date
      • Prepare licensing, background checks and compliance onboarding
